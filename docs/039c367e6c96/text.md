Section 3: Signal Head Equipment
This section deals with signal lights and signal heads, and with Transilluminated Signs
installed above the Signal Heads. The description of the tests and the relevant drawings
are included at the end of this section.
3.1 General
3.1.1 Introduction and Edition Control
Section 3 of the "General Specifications for the Placement and Maintenance of Signal
Lights in Israel" has been re-written and edited in 2011 (Edition 1 — in which this section
was numbered 3.2), in accordance with the European Standard EN 12368.
In year 2023 (Edition 6) the title of this Section was changed to: "Signal Head Equipment",
and its number was changed from 3.2 to 3, in accordance with structure of the updated
Signa Lights Specifications.
New requirements were added in this Section, in accordance with CLC/TS 50509:2007
Standard, regarding Signal Heads in LED technology. Document CLC/TS 50509:2007
incorporates requirements with regards to additional technical characteristics of LED
Signal Lights, which are not included in EN 12368:2015 Standard.
Additionally, Clause 3.16 was added - "Transilluminated Signs for Signal Heads". This
clause incorporates updated requirements for Transilluminated Signs that are installed
above Signal Heads. These requirements replace the requirements that were included in
Section 3.3 of the "General Specifications for the Placement and Maintenance of Signal
Lights in Israel" dated 1993.
Generally, all the requirements of EN 12368 are applicable in Israel. These requirements
refer to the optical requirements, such as signal head size, distribution of luminous
intensity, luminance uniformity, maximum phantom signal, colors of signal lights, and
background screen of signal lights. The luminous intensities of signal lights that are
required in Israel are higher than the requirement of EN 12368, as detailed in this
document.
This document includes Appendix A, that specifies the applicability of the European
Standards to Signal Heads in Israel.
Appendix A is divided into two parts:
« Appendix A-1: Applicability of the EN 12368:2015 Standard to Israeli Traffic Signal
Heads.
« Appendix A-2: Applicability of the CLC/TS 50509:2007 Standard to Israeli Traffic
Signal Heads.

3.1.2 General Requirements for Signal Heads
Signal heads systems shall be designed in accordance with the 1961 Traffic Regulations,
and with the Table of Signs, in its most updated version. The signal heads shall be of a
standard model, which is part of a production series approved by the Ministry of Transport.
The list of approved signal heads shall be as defined in the document: Traffic and Safety
Devices and Signal Heads Approved for Placement on the Road", in its most updated
version, and in accordance with the Guidelines for Traffic Signals Design, in their most
updated version, by the Ministry of Transport.
New signal lights installed in Israel shall be based on LED technology, or equivalent.
It is prohibited to install in Israel new signal heads with incandescent bulbs.
However, at intersections where (and only where) existing signal heads with incandescent
bulbs exist, it is allowed to use and install spare parts for this type of signal heads, and
incandescent bulbs. The requirements for Signal heads with Incandescent Bulb are
detailed Appendix C (not included in the English Edition).
It is prohibited to install in one intersection signal heads of different models and/or different
technologies. "One intersection" for this matter includes split or adjacent intersections, that
are operated by a single Traffic Signal Controller.
The signal head system for vehicle guidance shall include 3 signal lights, colored red,
yellow and green, diameter 300 mm (signs 701-706).
The signal head system for public transport guidance shall include 3 / 4 / 5 signal lights,
colored white, diameter 200 mm (signs 708 to 717).

3.2 Signal Head Dimensions and Weight
The dimensions of the signal lights are defined by the diameter of the front part of the lens,
and the maximal dimensions of the signal head housing.
3.2.1 The Vehicle Signal Head System
The vehicle head system shall be composed of 3 signal lights, with identical diameter, as
defined in Appendix A. Signal lights of different dimensions shall not be installed in the
same system. The maximal dimensions of the signal head system, including the
background screen, shall be as defined in Appendix A. The weight of the signal head
system, including the background screen, shall not exceed 20 kg.
3.2.2 The Public Transport Signal Head System
The public transport (PT) signal head system shall be composed of 3 signal lights, with
identical diameter, as defined in Appendix A. Signal lights of different dimensions shall
not be installed in the same system. The maximal dimensions of the signal head system,
including the background screen, shall be as defined in Appendix A. The weight of the
signal head system, including the background screen, shall not exceed 11 kg.
Sometimes the PT signal head system is composed of 4 or 5 signal lights (see: Design
Guidelines of Signal Heads for Mass Transit Systems, by the Ministry of Transport, 2008).
In such cases, the additional one or two signal lights shall be placed next to the bottom
signal light, and then the maximal dimension to be added for each signal light shall be
300X300 mm. The maximal weight to be added for each signal head shall be 3.5 kg. Signal
lights of different dimensions shall not be installed in the same system. See clause 3.11
regarding mechanical and wind load requirements.
3.2.3 Pedestrian Signal Head System
The pedestrian signal head system shall be composed of 2 signal lights, with identical
diameter, as defined in Appendix A. The maximal dimensions of the signal head system
shall be 60 X 30 cm. The weight of the signal head system shall not exceed 7 kg.

3.3 The Housing of the Signal Head
The housing of the Signal shall be a molded box of a rigid plastic material (polycarbonate),
self-extinguished, with a sidewall of at least 3 mm. The front side of the housing shall be
open and designated for a door, in which the signal head is fixed. The signal head shall
be made of a stabilized material against UV radiation, including the color of the housing.
The color of the signal head housing shall be black (RAL 9005) or dark green (RAL 6009),
as selected by the purchaser. The front housing of the signal head shall be black. The
external housing parts shall be without corners or sharp edges that might inflict injuries.
The finish of the external parts shall be opaque, in order to avoid reflections on the signal
head housing.
The housing of the signal head shall be a modular unit, which enables the assembly of
one, two or three signal lights as one unit.
The door shall be lockable and sealed by a neoprene ring. It shall also be closed using a
spring-switch or a screw, in a manner that would enable opening and closing the door
manually, without tools.
All metal components, such as screws, nuts, bolts and joints, shall be of galvanized metal
or suitable plastic material. Spring switches shall be replaceable in case of breaking or
becoming loose. The replacement shall not require pasting.
In the center of the floor and top end of the signal head, there shall be an opening with a
diameter of at least 3 cm, which shall enable the attachment of the signal head housings
and the passage of the electrical cables. In cases where the signal head system includes
4 or 5 signal lights, openings shall be implemented in the sidewalls of the signal heads, in
order to allow the horizontal attachment of the signal heads.
The signal heads housing shall be attached in a manner that prevents them from relative
rotation.

3.8.3 Definition of LED Signal Light Shutdown due to LED Burning
When Incandescent bulb is used in a signal light, the light source is a single bulb.
Consequently, when the bulb gets burned, the signal light shuts down, and it is clear that
is should be replaced.
However, when LEDs are used in a signal light, the light source is a few LED units. When
one (or more) LED units get burned, the signal light does not shut down, but stays on with
a reduced luminous intensity. In such a case, the signal light shall be designed in such a
way that when its luminous intensity is reduced and is under the specified level, the signal
