Clause 3.4:
Unified Acoustic Tactile Device (UATD)
1. Introduction
Acoustic Tactile Signal Device (hereinafter: ATSD) is designated to provide persons with
vision impairments, and persons with vision and hearing impairments, with acoustic and
tactile information regarding the pedestrian traffic signal instructions (red light / green
light).
Pedestrian Demand Unit (hereinafter: PDU) is a device allowing the identification of a
pedestrian demand for a green light in a certain crosswalk of the intersection. The PDU
transmits a request to the signal controller, for receiving a green light at that crosswalk,
as defined in the signal timing plan. Pressing the push button is stored in the traffic light
controller until the green signal for the required phase appears (‘a detector with
memory”). Pressing the push button during the green light is stored in the signal
controller.
ATSD, installed in the intersection, includes a push button, designated to activate a
“Vocal Message”. If the ATSD is installed in the median (central island) of a divided
roadway, the push button is also used for silencing the ATSD of the crosswalk that the
person with vision impairments has already passed.
Unified Acoustic Tactile Device (hereinafter: UATD) is designated to function as ATSD,
as described above, and also to identify the demand of pedestrians (with no vision and
hearing impairments) for a green light. Consequently, the push button of the UATD will
be used as a pedestrian demand push button for the crosswalk that the pedestrian did
not cross yet.
This specification replaces Clause 3.4 of the General Specifications for the Placement
and Maintenance of Traffic Signal Lights in Israel (1993).
UATD shall be installed in any new installation. This device shall be used both as
ATSD for persons with vision impairments and/or for persons with vision and
hearing impairments, and also shall allow its use as PDU (if necessary), in
accordance with this specification.
This specification shall become valid 6 months after its publication. During these 6
months, it shall be possible to submit application for approval of devices in accordance
with the exiting requirements, as well as in accordance with this new specification.

2. Requirements for ATSD based on the Israeli Standard
The requirements for ATSD are defined in Israeli Standard IS 5990.
Israeli Standard IS 5990-1 defines the functional requirements for ATSD, while Israeli
Standard 1S 5990-2 defines the installation and operational requirements for ATSD.
The Israeli Standard 1S 5990 requirements are valid, therefore this specification does
not deal with the requirements of the Israeli Standard, but adds requirements referring to
the integration of PDU with ATSD, as Unified Acoustic Tactile Device (UATD).
3. Additional Requirements for UATD
UATD shall be installed in any new installation. This device shall be used both as ATSD
for persons with vision impairments and/or for persons with vision and hearing
impairments, and also shall allow its use as PDU (if necessary), in accordance with this
specification.
General Requirements
The UATD that is manufactured in Israel or imported and implemented in Israel, has to
meet any law, standard, rule or regulation that are valid in Israel.
The UATD shall be a standard model, which is part of a production series.
It shall be convenient for operation and maintenance.
The body of the UATD shall be made of metal or rigid plastic material.
The color of the UATD shall be Yellow, or a combination of Yellow and Black.
When the UATD functions also as PDU, and the push button is activated, the UATD
shall present a feedback in the form of Illuminated Arrow, in the direction of the crossing,
colored White or Yellow on a Black background. The UATD shall also present a vocal
feedback, confirming to the user that the demand was received in the system (for
example: “Your Request was Recorded”). The illuminated feedback shall be present only
during the red-light phase of the associated crosswalk. The Illuminated Arrow shall be
visible in direct sun light.
The UATD push button, either used as PDU, or not, will be in one of the following
shapes:
*. Circle with a minimum diameter of 3+10% cm
* Rectangle with minimum dimensions of 3+10% א‎ 3+10% cm.
It is emphasized that the same ATSD push button is used for silencing the acoustic and
vocal messages (of the crosswalk that the person with vision impairments has already
passed), and also for the pedestrian demand for a green light (when the ATSD functions
as PDU).

As defined in the Israeli Standard IS 5990-1, the silencing function is activated following
a long pushing of 3 seconds or more. Consequently, the length of the button-pushing
duration shall be a configurable parameter, and shall have values of 3 seconds and
higher.
When the UATD also functions as PDU, pushing the button in any duration (also smaller
than 3 seconds), shall activate the pedestrian demand for a green light.
The push button shall transmit a signal to the traffic light controller for demanding a
green light at the associated crosswalk, as defined in the timing plan.
The Israeli Standard IS 5990 requires a Tactile Direction Indicator, showing the direction
of walking on the associated crosswalk, which protrudes at least 0.8 mm above the
UATD surface, and is at least 35 mm long. In addition to the IS 5990 requirements, it is
required that the Tactile Direction Indicator shall be coined on the ATSD surface.
When the UATD does not receive a green or red signal from the associated traffic light
pedestrian lamp, the UATD shall cease to function as such, except for its function as
PDU.
The UATD shall include a real-time clock, backed up by a battery or capacitor, with
backing up duration of at least 4 days.
The UATD shall allow the storage of timetable parameters in a non-volatile memory.
Vocal Signals
As defined in the IS 5990, the generation of Vocal Messages is optional. However, a
UATD with the ability to generate Vocal Signals shall implement a list of messages in
accordance with the required format as presented in Appendix C.
It shall be possible to defined a timetable for activating or blocking or amending the
recorded messages, and also changes in the voice volume.
The Vocal Messages shall be activated by pressing the push button, in any duration.
Mechanical and Electrical Requirements
The UATD shall comply with the requirements of the Israeli Electricity Law, 1954, and its
Regulations, and in particular, the Israeli Electrical Regulations for Guidance Signs
(Traffic Signal Lights), [In Hebrew: TAHARAM], 2001.
The components of the push button, that may come in contact with a human touch,
whether or not the UATD functions as a PDU, shall be implemented using non-
conductive electrically isolated materials.

The mains supply voltage of the UATD shall be using the same mains supply voltage
corresponding to the output voltage of the traffic light controller and lamp voltage. For
example, if the traffic light controller and the output for operating the signal heads are
using voltage of 48 Vac, the UATD shall also operate with supply voltage of 48 Vac.
The required UATD’s Degree of Protection shall comply with IP 54.
The external and cover components of the UATD, that are exposed to direct sunlight,
shall be resistant to UV Radiation. The manufacturer shall declare meeting this
requirement.
Marking Requirements
The following information shall be marked by carving the letters on a conspicuous
location on the body of the UATD:
*. Manufacturer's Name
« Type and Model of the UATD
e Sequence Number and Production Year
« Catalogue Number of the UATD.
The installation Requirements are described in Appendix B.

4. Testing and Approval of UATD
Guidelines for preparation of the Test Report are detailed below.
The UATD testing shall be conducted in a “Recognized Optical Laboratory”, as defined
by the procedure of the Inter-Ministerial Committee for the Examination and Approval of
Traffic and Safety Devices, in its latest edition.
All the tests and measurements shall be carried out in accordance with the testing
methods which are detailed in the !5 5990 and in this document.
The Test Report shall include a confirmation of meeting the requirements, issued by the
Recognized Laboratory, and also a detailed report of checking the requirements, clause-
by-clause.
The Test Report shall be prepared by a Recognized Laboratory which was certified by
the Israeli Laboratory Accreditation Authority (ILAA), or by a parallel Organization from
another country, that has a mutual recognition agreement with ILAA, in the area of:
Physical and Engineering Testing — Noise Measurement.
The Test Report of the Recognized Laboratory shall consist of two parts, as follows:
Part 1:
A report that follows the clauses of IS 5990, clause-by-clause:
*. All clauses of IS 5990 Part 1
e Clause 5.5 of IS 5990 Part 2.
Part 2:
A supplementary report that includes the additional requirements detailed in this
document, in Appendix A below.
The following should be attached to the Test Report:
*. Color photographs in good quality of the tested UATD, from all angles and in all
states (for example when the button is pressed).
* Catalogue Numbers of the device.
The laboratory shall sign the Test Report with original stamp and signature.
