General

The EN 12368 European Standard applies to Red, Yellow and Green signal lights, for
road traffic with 200 mm and 300 mm roundels.

The Table below specifies the applicability of the EN 12368 standard to the Israeli
signal lights.

A clause can be:
    • Fully Applicable – All of the EN 12368 requirements in this clause are
       applicable
    • Applicable, Required Class X - The EN 12368 requirements in this clause are
       applicable, but when a few classes are defined, only the specified Class is
       required in Israel.
    • Fully Applicable (with comments) - All of the EN 12368 requirements in this
       clause are applicable, but the comment explains the applicability to Israel.
    • Modified - The EN 12368 requirements in this clause are applicable with
       modification. The modification is detailed.

EN 12368 is also applicable to Public Transport signal lights (White) in Israel, with
modifications to Clause 6 – Optical Requirements.
