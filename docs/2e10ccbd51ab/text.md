A.1 General
This annex suggests parameters and a calculation method for determining the effective
height of an anti-glare system that is needed to block out the incident rays which may dazzle
the driver of an oncoming vehicle.
The calculation given in clause A.3 is based on the path of the light ray which just avoids
being blocked out by the system
The fact that such a ray may not be part of an incident light beam of a vehicle (excessive
vertical beam angle, see clause A.5) is not considered
The beam of vehicle headlamps generally illuminates up to approximately 15° above the
road surface.
The minimum effective heights which are calculated may, because of this, be slightly greater
than the true minimum effective heights
In practice, the following calculation provides a good approximation in the case of pavements
with a constant longitudinal gradient. In specific cases (sag or summit curves) it is necessary
to perform a separate calculation
