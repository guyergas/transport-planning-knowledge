1. Introduction
The Ministry of Transport and Road Safety (hereinafter: "MOT") initiates a Testing and
Approval Procedure for Traffic Signal Planning Software (hereinafter: "TSPS").
Traffic Regulation 16 (2), 1961 (hereinafter: "The Regulations") authorize the Chief Transport
Commissioner in MOT (hereinafter: "The Commissioner") to set traffic arrangements. In order
to exercise this authority, The Commissioner has appointed “Advisory Committee on Traffic
Signal Planning Software”, designated to recommend to The Commissioner on the approval
of TSPS (hereinafter: "The Committee").
This Third Edition of the Procedure was updated in accordance with the MOT Guidelines for
Traffic Signal Deign (June 2025). The design of the Traffic Signals that is implemented using
the TSPS, shall be in accordance with the up-to-date Design Guidelines, including all updates
that will be published for the Guidelines, if published. The technical requirements for the TSPS
are detailed in Appendix 1.
2. The Authority of The Committee
The authority of The Committee is to examine, on behalf of MOT, the compliance of TSPS
with MOT requirements, and to recommend to The Commissioner on the approval or rejection
of the proposed TSPS.
3. The Purpose of The Committee
The purpose of The Committee is to regulate the submission of TSPS for approval, and to
regulate the TSPS testing procedure, and the recommendation procedure to The
Commissioner חס‎ the approval / rejection of TSPS.
4. Terminology
In this Procedure -
e "דסא"‎ - Israeli Ministry of Transport and Road Safety.
e "The Committee” — Advisory Committee on Traffic Signal Planning Software.
e "Recommendation on the approval of TSPS" — The recommendation to The
Commissioner on the approval of the Traffic Signal Planning Software (TSPS), after
which the approval of The Commissioner is granted, and TSPS can be used for the
planning of traffic signal plans that are submitted for approval in Israel.

5. Members of The Committee
The Committee includes the chairperson, and additional representatives of MOT, and
representatives of Local Signage Authorities responsible for most of the signalized road
network in Israel, and representatives of additional relevant organizations, as detailed in the
Power of Appointment.
The chairperson is the Head of Traffic Engineering Department in MOT.
The Committee shall convene from time to time, as required.
6. Procedure for Examination and Approval of New TSPS
MOT receives applications for approval and for re-approval for usage in Israel of TSPS. The
software version to be submitted for approval is the version designated for usage in Israel.
6.1. The Application for Approval of New TSPS shall be Submitted as Detailed
Below:
6.1.1. The Software Supplier — the applicant - shall submit the following
documentation:
6.1.1.1. Application Form for the approval of a new TSPS, Forms A-E,
included in Appendix 2.
6.1.1.2. As required in Appendix 1.
6.1.1.3. The Testing Forms included in Appendix 1, Supplement C,
signed by “The Consultant” and by the Software Supplier (see
also Clause 6.2.1).

6.1.1.4. Most updated operational documents, and documentation of
TSPS versions, including the documentation of variations
implemented in the recent version.
6.1.1.5. MOT is entitled to request any additional documentation or

6.1.3. The Application shall be submitted to MOT by email, to an address

material shall be submitted in two computerized copies on a flash drive,

6.1.4. The applicant shall submit full and correct answers for every question in

Committee's request.
6.1.5. All documentation shall be submitted in Hebrew or English only.
Documents whose original language is not Hebrew or English shall be
submitted in their original language, enclosed with a notary-approved
translation to Hebrew or English.
6.2. Procedure for Examination and Approval of a New TSPS
6.2.1. The Software Supplier shall employ, on his/her own expense, Traffic Signal
Designer with experience in Traffic Signal Design (hereinafter: The
Consultant"). The Consultant shall act as Professional Escort for the Software
Supplier in the entire TSPS approval process, including testing the
compliance of the TSPS with the Appendix 1 requirements. The Consultant
shall fill in the Testing Forms included in Appendix 1, Supplement C, and shall
approve them by his/her signature. These forms shall be submitted by the
Software Supplier, as part of the application. The Consultant shall be
committed to re-testing the new TSPS as required, as a result of the TSPS
functioning according to the requirements of this Procedure, and as a result of
the comments given by the MOT Examiners, as detailed in Clause 6.2.5
below. As a response to every round of comments of the MOT Examiners,
and to the TSPS update as required, the Consultant shall be committed to
submit updated Testing Forms, approved and signed.
6.2.2. At the request of the Committee, the Software Supplier shall install the TSPS,
for the purpose of examination, on at least 3 different computers. The
Software Supplier shall allocate resources, as required, to support the
examination process, including the provision of clarifications with regards to
the operation of the TSPS.
6.2.3. The Software Supplier shall conduct, on his/her own expense, training
sessions for the TSPS MOT Examiners, as required.
6.2.4. MOT, through The Committee and the examiners, shall examine the new
TSPS, using the procedure defined in the document: "The MOT
Requirements for Traffic Signal Planning Software", in the most recent
version published in the MOT Website (defined as Appendix 1).
6.2.5. MOT shall appoint two Examiners on its behalf, for the TSPS testing
(hereinafter: "The MOT Examiners").
6.2.6. The MOT Examiners shall test the compliance of the TSPS with the
requirements detailed in Appendix 1.
6.2.7. The MOT Examiners shall prepare (each one separately) a TSPS Testing
Report, and the reports shall be distributed by MOT to The Committee
members.
6.2.8. The Committee shall hold a discussion for the purpose of recommendation on
the approval or rejection of the TSPS proposed in the application.

6.2.9. The representatives of the TSPS Supplier and the representatives of the
organization ordering the TSPS (if there is one) shall be entitled to participate
in the Committee's meetings, in the stage of the TSPS presentation. However,
they shall not be entitled to participate in the stage of the internal discussions
and decision making regarding the TSPS approval.
6.2.10. The Committee chairperson, or acting chairperson, shall present The
Committee's recommendations to The Commissioner for the purpose of
receiving his approval.
6.2.11. MOT, through The Committee and/or The Examiners, shall issue an approval
for using the TSPS, signed by The Commissioner, by virtue of his authority to
approve traffic arrangements [Traffic Regulation 16 (2)]. MOT shall publish
the new TSPS, approved by The Commissioner, as compliant with the MOT
requirements, as part of the List of Traffic Signal Planning Software.
6.2.12. The Software Supplier is required to inform The Committee with regards to
any variation implemented in the TSPS, and any new version that is
distributed. If a new version that does not have The Commissioner's approval
is distributed, MOT shall not accept traffic signal plans in this version. The
procedure for approval an existing TSPS is described in Clause 7 below.
6.2.13. The Software Supplier is required to adapt the TSPS to comply with any
update of MOT Guidelines. If an updated TSPS is not submitted for approval
within 90 days, as required by this Procedure, and following explicit written
request by The Committee / MOT, MOT is entitled, through The Committee, to
delete the TSPS from the List of approved Traffic Signal Planning Software.
6.2.14. The Committee shall establish an email address, or any other
communications means, through which complaints with regards to the TSPS
operation can be received.

. When existing approval is about to expire,

. When the TSPS has been updated following an update of MOT Guidelines,
*. When an updated version of TSPS was submitted for approval, as initiated by the
Software Supplier.

7.1. When Existing Approval is about to Expire
7.1.1. When existing approval is about to expire, and as much as the TSPS has not
changed, and the circumstances described in Clauses 7.2 to 7.4 below are not
applicable, the Software Supplier shall submit an Application for extension of an
Approval of an approved TSPS, six months prior the expiration of the existing
approval. The Application shall be in accordance with Clause 6.1 above,
excluding the requirements of Sub-Clauses 6.1.1.1 to 6.1.1.4, Instead, only
Forms A-D in Appendix 2 shall be submitted.
7.1.2. In such circumstances re-testing of approved TSPS in not required.
7.2. When Reports or Complaints on TSPS Malfunction and Actual Usage are
Received
7.2.1. When reports or complaints on TSPS malfunction and actual usage are
received, The Committee is authorized to decide upon the extent of the
required re-testing, including full re-testing (as defined in Clause 6.2 above),
or partial re-testing, for the purpose of examining the complaints.
7.2.2. If non-compliance with MOT requirements is found, the Software Supplier
shall be required to implement the required amendments, and submit an
updated version of the TSPS for re-testing — full or partial, upon The
Committee's decision.
7.3. When MOT Guidelines have been Updated and became valid following the
TSPS Approval (including an Update of the Unified Format)
7.3.1. Upon MOT request, the Software Supplier shall submit documentation and
professional material with regards to the extent of compliance of the TSPS
with the updated MOT Guidelines, within 30 days from the date of MOT
request.
7.3.2. The Software Supplier shall submit an updated version of the TSPS, that is
compliant with the new MOT Guidelines, within 90 days from the date of
publication of MOT Guidelines change.
7.3.3. The procedure for re-testing and approval of an approved TSPS shall be
similar to the procedure for approval of a new TSPS, as detailed in Clauses
6.1, 6.2 above, including the requirement mentioned in Clause 6.2.1 above.
7.3.4. The usage of the approved version of TSPS (without the implementation of
the update of MOT Guidelines) shall be permitted for a transition period, as
defined in the new guidelines, if defined.
7.3.5. If the Software Supplier does not submit for approval a new version of the
TSPS, the approval of the TSPS shall expire at the end of the transition
period, as defined in the new guidelines, if defined.
7.4. When an updated version of TSPS was Submitted for Approval, as Initiated by
the Software Supplier
7.4.1. If the Software Supplier has initiated the issuance of an updated version of
the TSPS, the Software Supplier shall submit this updated version, for
approval.
7.4.2. The Software Supplier shall submit a declaration, detailing the variations
included in the new version.

7.4.3. MOT is authorized to decide upon the extent of the required re-testing,
including full re-testing, as defined in Clauses 6.1, 6.2 above, for the purpose
of approving the updated version of TSPS.
7.4.4. MOT is not obligated to approve the updated version, and MOT is entitled to

7.5. In all cases described in Clauses 7.2-7.4, the application for approval of an approved

7.6. In all cases described in Clauses 7.2 to 7.4 above, for which full or partial re-testing

submission of Testing Forms signed by The Consultant, as described in Clause 6.2.1

. The Approval Validity, its Extension, Cancellation, and Additional
Conditions
8.1. The validity of the approval is for a period of no more than 3 years. As much as the
validity is provided in stages, the total length of approval periods shall not be longer
than 3 years.
8.2. The Committee is authorized to recommend on providing an approval for less than 3
years.
8.3. When an application for the extension of approval of an approved TSPS is not
submitted, following an examination of the necessity of extension and TSPS usage,
The Committee is entitled to recommend granting an extension of the TSPS approval
validity, as detailed above.
8.4. The Committee is entitled to recommend the approval cancelation, suspension, or
conditioning, and adding / canceling / changing the conditions for approval.
8.5. The Committee is entitled to recommend the deletion of a TSPS that has not been
used in the past 6 years.
8.6. The Committee is entitled to require from the TSPS applicant to submit additional
information, upon its own discretion.
8.7. The Committee is entitled to conduct re-testing of the operation in practice of TSPS
that was approved, for the purpose of problem finding.
8.8. When malfunction is detected in TSPS, a discussion of the TSPS shall be held by
The Committee, also before the approval expires, and the TSPS may be deleted
from the List of Traffic Signal Planning Software, if necessary.
. TSPS Approval
9.1. Following The Committee's recommendation, and subject to the approval of The
Commissioner, The Commissioner shall sign the approval for using the TSPS in
Israel.
9.2. The TSPS approval shall be published from time to time, as part of the List of Traffic
Signal Planning Software, in the MOT Website.

10.INBAR Software
10.1. It is emphasized that INBAR Software, that was developed by MOT, and is being
used by many public agencies, is not required to comply with the requirements of this
Procedure. INBAR shall continue to be utilized for traffic signal design in Israel,
including amendments that will be implemented (if implemented) in the future, subject
to the decisions of MOT, as long as the Ministry of Transport approves it.
